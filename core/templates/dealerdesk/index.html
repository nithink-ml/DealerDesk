Forecasting Methodology & Validation

Trained on 26-week rolling-origin validation windows without data leakage. The model selection algorithm achieves an aggregate Weighted Absolute Percentage Error (WAPE) of 0.362, outperforming naive 13-week moving average (0.396) and last-value baseline (0.535).

Safety Stock = ceil(1.65 × σ × √LeadTime)
Reorder Point = Lead Time Demand + Safety Stock
Reorder Qty = max(0, ceil(Reorder Point - Live Stock))
